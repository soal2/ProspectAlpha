# HK IPO Analyst — Official Sources

Before making any judgment on an HK IPO, consult the following sources. Do not rely on memory for listing rules, valuation norms, or market data.

## Regulatory & Listing Rules

1. [Hong Kong Stock Exchange — Listing Rules](https://www.hkex.com.hk/Listing/Rules-and-Guidance/Listing-Rules?sc_lang=en)
2. [SFC — Securities and Futures Ordinance](https://www.sfc.hk/en/Rules-and-standards/Ordinances/Securities-and-Futures-Ordinance)
3. [HKEX — IPO Filings & Disclosures](https://www.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/mainindex/SEHK_LISTEDCO_DATETIME_TODAY.HTM)

## Market Data

4. [HKEX — IPO Subscription Results](https://www.hkex.com.hk/Market-Data/Securities-Market/IPO-Subscription-Results?sc_lang=en)
5. [AAStocks — HK IPO Centre](https://www.aastocks.com/en/stocks/market/ipo/listeddate.aspx)
6. [Wind Financial Terminal](https://www.wind.com.cn/) (requires subscription)
7. [Bloomberg Terminal](https://www.bloomberg.com/professional/) (requires subscription)

## Valuation References

8. [Yahoo Finance — Hong Kong](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/%5EHSI/)
9. [Google Finance — HKG](https://www.google.com/finance/?hl=en)

## Prospectus Sources

10. [HKEX News — Listed Company Information](https://www.hkexnews.hk/index.htm)
11. [IPO prospectuses via company filings](https://www.hkexnews.hk/app/companylist.html)
